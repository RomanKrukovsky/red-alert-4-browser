The five evaluated Sketchfab candidates are not downloaded. The official download API requires `SKETCHFAB_OAUTH_TOKEN`; their records remain `BLOCKED_AUTH` in `assets/licenses/asset-manifest.json`.

Detailed source URLs, hashes, processing notes, triangle counts and runtime hashes are stored in `assets/licenses/asset-manifest.json`.
